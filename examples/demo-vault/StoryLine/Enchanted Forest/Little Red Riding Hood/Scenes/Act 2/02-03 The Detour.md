# The Detour

Little Red Riding Hood sets the basket in the grass and gathers bellflowers,
daisies, and red clover. Behind every tree she discovers an even lovelier bloom
and wanders farther from the path. Meanwhile, the wolf runs directly to
Grandmother's house without a detour.
