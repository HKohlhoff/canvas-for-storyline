# The Voice in the Bed

The front door stands open. In the dark bedroom, a figure lies beneath a cap
and blanket. Little Red Riding Hood wonders at her supposed Grandmother's ears,
eyes, and hands. When she asks about the large mouth, the disguised wolf leaps
from the bed and swallows the child as well.
