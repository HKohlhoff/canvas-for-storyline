# The Huntsman Hears Snoring

The huntsman stops outside the house because the snoring within makes the
windowpanes tremble. He recognizes the wolf in the bed. Carefully, he opens the
wolf's belly and frees Little Red Riding Hood first, then Grandmother. Together,
they fill it with stones; when the wolf wakes, he can no longer escape.
