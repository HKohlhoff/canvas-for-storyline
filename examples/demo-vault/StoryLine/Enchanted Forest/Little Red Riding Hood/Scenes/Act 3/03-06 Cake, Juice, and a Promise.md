# Cake, Juice, and a Promise

Grandmother regains her strength with cake and juice. The huntsman rests at the
table, and Little Red Riding Hood recounts the encounter in the forest. She
promises herself never again to leave the safe path for a beautiful distraction.
That afternoon, she walks home with care.
