# The Errand

Early in the morning, Mother places a basket on the table. Inside are a piece
of cake and a bottle of currant juice for the ailing Grandmother. Little Red
Riding Hood promises not to leave the forest path or dawdle along the way. She
then puts on the red hood and sets out.
