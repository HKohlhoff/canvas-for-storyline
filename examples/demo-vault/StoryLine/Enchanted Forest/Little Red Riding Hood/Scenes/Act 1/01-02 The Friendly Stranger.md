# The Friendly Stranger

A wolf steps onto the path between tall fir trees. Little Red Riding Hood does
not fear him and innocently answers his questions. When the wolf learns that
Grandmother lives alone at the far end of the forest, he forms a plan. Smiling,
he points out the flowers beside the path.
